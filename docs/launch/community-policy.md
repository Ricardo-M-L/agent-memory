# Launch channels and publication boundaries

Checked on 2026-09-06. Recheck rules before a future submission.

- V2EX: personal independent work belongs in 分享创造; company marketing belongs in 推广.
  [Node guide](https://www.v2ex.com/help/node). `posts.zh-CN.md` is a draft until a topic URL is verified.
- Rust Chinese community: use a relevant project/technical category and an authenticated author
  account. No bulk reposting, invented community identity or credential extraction.
- This Week in Rust: technical walkthroughs and concrete CFP issues can be proposed through a
  draft PR. Plain Project/Tooling Updates no longer accept PR submissions. LLM-written articles
  must disclose authorship; this project's walkthrough does.
  [Submission rules](https://github.com/rust-lang/this-week-in-rust/blob/main/README.md).
- Hacker News: the author should write their own introduction and replies. Do not paste
  AI-generated or AI-edited comments, ask others for votes, or auto-engage.
  [Guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html),
  [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html).
- r/rust: moderators may remove submissions appearing AI-generated. No auto-posting of this
  launch copy. The maintainer may independently write a useful technical account consistent
  with the [community rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/rust/about/rules.json).

GitHub stars, traffic and clones are observational signals, not verified users or a causal
attribution system. A submission is not acceptance; a draft is not publication. No paid ads,
star exchanges, fake endorsements or unsolicited bulk messages are part of this launch.
